Supplementary Material

Where the Bits Must Live: A Tight Input-Side Information Bound
for Fixed-State Spectral Models



Anonymous submission. This document contains the appendices to the
main paper. It is self-contained: each proof restates the final
premises and the identifier of the claim it establishes, and proofs
cross-reference those identifiers rather than referring to 'the
proof above'. Numerical witnesses certify non-vacuity and
agreement; they are never used as premises.
