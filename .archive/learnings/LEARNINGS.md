### Summary
User clarified that 《安魂曲：新元》 is their own authored work and is authorized for training.

### Details
I initially treated the novel as a third-party copyrighted work and excluded it. The correct handling is to include it when the user states they are the author/rightsholder and grants training permission, while recording it as author-owned authorized data in the dataset manifest.

### Suggested Action
Create a separate source manifest for the novel corpus with authorization note and keep it traceable from public web corpora.
